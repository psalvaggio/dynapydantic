    dynapydantic Benchmarks¶

    This page shows the results of local benchmarking of this library. Absolute performance is largely governed by pydantic-core, so the figures reported here are measurments over a hand-rolled equivalent solution.

    Legend¶

    • MC = Model-construction time union realization
    • VT = Validation-time union realization
    • Disc = Discriminated union
    • Smart = Smart union
